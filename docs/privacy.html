Bingo Boy privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

Bingo Boy does not collect, send or share any personal information.

Photos and bingo cards. Photos you take or choose, and the numbers read from them, are stored only on your device. Reading the numbers happens on your device; photos are never uploaded.

Camera and photo library. The app asks for the camera and photo library only so you can photograph or choose pictures of your bingo pages.

Backups. A backup file is created only when you ask for one, and goes only where you choose to save or send it.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The app has no sign-in, shows no ads, and contains no analytics or tracking.

Deleting your data. Use "Clear all books" in the app, or delete the app, to remove everything it stored.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/PPP12AG/Bingo-Boy.
